Education




I was raised by my mother, a single parent, together with my two siblings. I completed my education from elementary to senior high school in private schools, something I am deeply grateful for because of my mother’s hard work and dedication.


She has always been committed not only to my needs but also to the needs of my siblings. Despite the challenges of being a single parent, she made sure that we were provided with quality education and a better.


Up until now, my mother continues to work hard overseas to support our family. Her sacrifices, perseverance, and love serve as my greatest motivation to stay focused, responsible, and determined in life.
