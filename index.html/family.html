MOM




I come from a close-knit family of three siblings, raised by my mother who is a single parent. Despite the challenges, she has worked hard to support and guide us in our daily lives.


At present, my mother is working overseas to provide for our future and ensure that we have better opportunities in life. Her sacrifices have greatly inspired me to stay responsible, focused, and determined in my studies and personal goals. As her child, I strive to make her efforts worthwhile by continuing to work hard, improve myself, and pursue a better future for our family.
